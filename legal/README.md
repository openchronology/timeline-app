The hosted service's policies and the operator's launch materials, for timescale.info and as a starting point for other installations. They are drafts, pending the operator details listed in `LAUNCH.md`. The build embeds `TERMS.md`, `PRIVACY.md`, `COPYRIGHT.md` and `LAUNCH.md` in the app's legal notices and `dist/legal.html` ([`scripts/legal.mjs`](../scripts/legal.mjs)), and the development server rebuilds the page when they change. Software licensing is separate: see [licensing](../docs/licensing.md), `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.

## Contents

| File                                       | What it is for                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [`COPYRIGHT.md`](COPYRIGHT.md)             | How to report copyright infringement, abuse and security problems to the operator (draft).        |
| [`LAUNCH.md`](LAUNCH.md)                   | The operator's checklist of details and obligations to settle before the hosted service launches. |
| [`PRIVACY.md`](PRIVACY.md)                 | The hosted service's privacy notice: what is collected, why, and for how long (draft).            |
| [`RISK-ASSESSMENT.md`](RISK-ASSESSMENT.md) | A template for the operator's online safety risk assessments, such as the UK Online Safety Act's. |
| [`TERMS.md`](TERMS.md)                     | The hosted service's terms of service (draft).                                                    |
