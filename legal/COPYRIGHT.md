# Copyright, abuse, and security reporting

Draft version: 2026-10-06. Operator: Athan Clark, an individual.
Public reporting email and, if used, designated-agent details are pending.
This draft does not assert that a DMCA agent has been registered or that a safe
harbor automatically applies. Reports cannot yet be submitted through an in-app
form; the published operator contact will be the reporting channel.

For a copyright complaint, identify the copyrighted work, the specific timeline,
plugin, comment, image, or URL at issue, your contact information, and why the use
is unauthorized. For a US DMCA notice, also include an electronic or physical
signature, a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized by the owner,
agent, or law, and a statement of accuracy and authority under penalty of perjury.
Do not include private account credentials. If you are uncertain whether the use
is permitted, including by license or fair use, assess that before sending a claim.

The operator should review complete reports promptly, restrict identified material
when appropriate, and notify the affected user unless prohibited or unsafe.
Report details may be shared with that user to allow a response; sensitive contact
information should be limited to what the process requires. Repeat infringers'
hosted accounts may be terminated in appropriate circumstances. Preserve a limited
record of decisions and respect lawful preservation requirements.

If US DMCA procedures apply, an affected user may provide a counter-notice with a
signature, identification of the removed material and its former location, a
statement under penalty of perjury of a good-faith belief that removal resulted
from mistake or misidentification, their name/address/telephone number, consent
to the relevant US federal court's jurisdiction, and acceptance of service from
the complainant or agent. The operator must follow applicable notification and
restoration procedures; restoration generally follows the statutory 10–14 business
day period after a valid counter-notice unless notice of a qualifying court action
is received. This procedure must be finalized with the actual designated agent.

For other abuse, provide specific URLs and a factual description of the concern.
For vulnerabilities, report privately with reproducible steps and minimal evidence.
Do not publish secrets, exploit unrelated accounts, or interrupt the service.
There is no promised bug bounty or blanket authorization for intrusive testing.

Software licensing complaints should identify the distributed version and the
missing license, source, or attribution. GPL rights remain available independently
of any account moderation decision.

Reference: https://www.copyright.gov/512/index.html and
https://copyright.gov/dmca-directory/ describe US procedures and agent designation.
