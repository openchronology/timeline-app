# OpenChronology — Tour: A Story's Timeline. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~1:53 (the scratch narration's cut) · **Format:** the real app, captured, in a browser frame, with infographic callouts; **Parchment** look · **Aspect:** 16:9
**Audience:** novelists, screenwriters, worldbuilders and game masters: people keeping an invented world's chronology straight across drafts, books or campaigns. They care about continuity, their own calendar, and keeping unpublished work private.
**Purpose:** show one invented world (the sample "The Ember Crown") the way a writer uses it: the world's long history in its own calendar, the saga up close, each character's thread pulled out, foreshadowing linked to its payoff, a revision that moves a whole thread at once, and where the work is kept.

**How it differs:** it speaks the audience's language (canon, threads, foreshadowing, continuity, in-world calendar) and never mentions real history. The world is original, made for this sample.

**Production notes:**

- Narration is 264 words, about 140 wpm when read. A storyteller's warmth; let the world's names breathe.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/saga.ochx`** (`saga.mjs`): years After the Reckoning (AR) as the time display's unit; Ash's thread in ember diamonds, Mirelle's in blue circles, the Hollow King's in violet squares, the world's events in gold (Moment colors and Moment shapes); chapter headings for the ages.
- **Accuracy guardrails:**
  - The calendar is the time display: a unit ("AR") with a scale of one Julian year and an origin at the Reckoning. Any unit and origin can be configured; months and weekdays of an invented calendar aren't modelled (say "years", not "your full calendar").
  - **Shift or scale times…** moves events with or without some tags, or what's related to one; one Undo reverses it in the browser.
  - Server timelines start private, but aren't hidden from the server's administrators; the desktop app keeps everything in files on your computer.

## Appendix C · Questions this audience is likely to ask

1. **Can I keep the story's order and the world's order separately?** Keep a second timeline for chapters, and **Compare** them on one axis; or tag scenes by chapter and separate by tags.
2. **Months, seasons, a 13-month year?** The built-in displays count units from an origin; a custom printer and parser can be written in **Time display** for a calendar of your own.
3. **Can my players see it without spoilers?** Share a separate public timeline with what they know; keep the full one private.
4. **Pictures of characters and places?** **Moment icons** and **Duration backgrounds**; link images you may share.
