The explainer video series: who it's for, a script and storyboard for each video, and the source that renders them. Start with [the audiences and the series](0-audiences-and-series.md), which says why there are ten videos and how each one is made for its audience. Each script gives every scene's picture (`FRAME`), movement (`MOTION`), on-screen text (`OST`) and narration (`VO`), then the narration alone for the voice session, the claims with their sources, and the questions the audience will ask.
