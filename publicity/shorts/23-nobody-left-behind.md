# OpenChronology — Short 23: Nobody Left Behind. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:45 · **Shape:** the zoom · **Look:** Archive · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** Marines, Marine veterans and their families, and military-history feeds. They know "Chosin" as a word on a sticker; what stops them is seeing the two weeks on a phone, day by day, and the choices that brought the wounded out.
**Purpose:** the second of the Marine Corps lessons series (Shorts 22–27). This one: **bring your people home.** It walks the Chosin Reservoir campaign, 27 November – 11 December 1950, through Fox Company's hill, Ray Davis's night march and the Hagaru-ri airstrip.
**Points to:** the Marine Corps timeline, `timescale.info/athan/usmc`.

**Production notes:**

- About 112 words (the numbers spelled out), read at about 150 wpm. The voice is the same as Short 22's: a retired senior NCO, plain and steady. Not heroic music-trailer energy; men died here, on both sides.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/usmc.ochx`**, shown in `phoneShot`, captured narrow as in Short 22 (`"size": [360, 640], "scale": 3`, **Group within** at 12 px). Views are given as dates (UTC).
- **New moments and durations this short needs** (added to the timeline before capture, each with its source):
  - "Task Force Faith east of the reservoir" (duration, 27 November – 1 December 1950: the Army's RCT-31, 7th Infantry Division);
  - "Fox Company holds Toktong Pass" (duration, 28 November – 2 December 1950: Capt. William E. Barber, 2nd Battalion, 7th Marines);
  - "Ray Davis leads 1/7 over the mountains to Fox Company" (1 December 1950);
  - "The airstrip at Hagaru-ri opens" (1 December 1950);
  - "117 dead are buried at Koto-ri" (8 December 1950);
  - "The air-dropped bridge at Funchilin Pass" (9 December 1950);
  - "The last ships leave Hungnam" (24 December 1950).
- **The readouts are drawn by the infographic layer** beside the phone, never over the app's text: a temperature chip, a "5 nights" bracket, an "8 miles" chip, a "4,000+" counter and a "78 miles" bracket.
- **Accuracy guardrails:**
  - The campaign was not the Marines' alone. The Army's RCT-31 (Task Force Faith), east of the reservoir, was overrun and lost more than a thousand men, and Britain's 41 (Independent) Commando fought alongside the Marines. Its band is on screen in Scene 1, and the pinned comment says so. Never say "the Marines alone".
  - "Thirty below": the cold was estimated as low as −36 °F (−38 °C).
  - Fox Company: "two hundred and twenty", and "eighty-two walk off that hill", both from Barber's Medal of Honor citation. (The 246 count includes attached corpsmen and others; we follow the citation.)
  - Davis: "eight miles", "at night", "in a snowstorm" and "carried out all his wounded" (22 of them stretcher cases), all from his Medal of Honor citation.
  - Hagaru-ri: "more than four thousand wounded" flown out over about six days (4,608 wounded and 81 dead, by the Air Force Association's count).
  - Koto-ri: 117 Marines, soldiers and Royal Marines commandos were buried there in one grave on 8 December. Sources differ on how many of the dead the columns carried out, so the short claims only what's documented.
  - "Seventy-eight miles": the road from the reservoir to Hungnam.
  - Smith's "attacking in a different direction" is not quoted: his exact words are reported several ways. It stays as the timeline's own moment, in view in Scene 5.

Above it, the headline, whole at frame 0; under it, in the look's serif, "Chosin Reservoir · 1950". A small chip beside the phone: "−36 °F".

**OST:** "Nobody left behind"

**VO:**
"November 1950. At the Chosin Reservoir, the 1st Marine Division is surrounded, at thirty below."

### Scene 2 · 0:05 – 0:12 · The pass

**FRAME:** The zoom runs in to 26 November – 4 December 1950: "Fox Company holds Toktong Pass" across the phone. Beside the phone, a bracket drawn to the band's length: "5 nights". Then, from a view starting 1 January 1950, the band opens in the reader: its dates, picture and notes.

**MOTION:** A continuous zoom easing to a stop; the bracket draws on "five nights"; the reader slides up.

**OST:** "Fox Company: 5 nights"

**VO:**
"The only road out runs through Toktong Pass. Fox Company holds it for five nights. Of two hundred and twenty men, eighty-two walk off that hill."
