# OpenChronology — Short 11: Lincoln Was President 1,503 Days. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:42 · **Shape:** the stat · **Look:** Archive · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** history and "did you know" feeds: curious newcomers and students. They know Lincoln and the Civil War as two facts; what stops them is a number that shows how completely one swallowed the other.
**Purpose:** one overlap made visible: Lincoln's presidency and the Civil War as two bands on a phone, nearly the same length, then the two short gaps at either end, zoomed into until they're days. It ends on the timeline's address.
**Points to:** Video 14, the Abraham Lincoln tour.

**Production notes:**

- About 95 words (the numbers spelled out), read briskly at about 170 wpm. The voice is a history teacher who has just done the sum and can't quite believe it.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/lincoln.ochx`**, captured at phone size (480 × 640, scale 2.25) and shown in `phoneShot`, with **Separate by tags** on `war`: the Civil War band and the battles lift into the upper track, and the President of the United States band stays in the lower one, on the same time axis.
- The counters and bars beside the phone are drawn by the infographic layer; the bands themselves are the app's.
- **Accuracy guardrails:** the war is counted from Fort Sumter (April 12, 1861) to Lee's surrender at Appomattox (April 9, 1865), the conventional span; the narration says "until Lee surrendered", and Appendix B notes the later surrenders. Day counts are differences between calendar dates (Appendix B shows the arithmetic); "97 percent" is rounded down from 97.0.

## ACT 2 · THE GAPS (0:16 – 0:36)

### Scene 3 · 0:16 – 0:26 · Thirty-nine days

**FRAME:** The phone zooms into 1861-02-20 → 1861-05-01: in the lower track, First inaugural address (03-04) and the start of the presidency band; in the upper track, Fort Sumter (04-12) and the Civil War band beginning. A bracket between them beside the phone: "39 days".

**MOTION:** A continuous zoom from the four years to the ten weeks; the bracket draws on "thirty-nine".

**OST:** "39 days of peace"

**FRAME:** The phone pans to 1865-03-25 → 1865-04-20: the Civil War band ending at Surrender at Appomattox (04-09) in the upper track, the presidency band ending a few days later in the lower one, with the summary of April 14–15 between. A bracket beside the phone: "6 days".

**MOTION:** A pan along the line (the same span), then the bracket draws on "six".

**OST:** "6 days after"

**VO:**
"And at the end, six. Lee surrendered on April the ninth. Lincoln was shot on the fourteenth, and died the next morning."
