# OpenChronology — Short 5: Check One Character's Thread. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:32 · **Shape:** the zoom · **Look:** Parchment · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** writers and worldbuilders: novelists with a cast of point-of-view characters, game masters, people with a series bible in a spreadsheet. They don't want software; they want their problem named, and a fix they can picture using tonight.
**Purpose:** name the problem (many characters, one story: does each thread hold up?), and show the fix on a phone: the world in its own calendar, then one character's thread pulled into a track of its own, then moved a year in one step.
**Points to:** Video 6, a story's timeline.

**Production notes:**

- About 85 words, a writer talking to writers: warm, practical.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/saga.ochx`** ("The Ember Crown", made up for these videos), captured at phone size and shown in `phoneShot`, with **Separate by tags** on `mirelle`. A small note on screen in Scene 2 says it's an invented world.
- The shift is drawn over the phone (the **Shift or scale times…** dialog, as in Video 6), not captured.

## ACT 2 · THE FIX (0:05 – 0:27)

### Scene 2 · 0:05 – 0:12 · Your world, your calendar

**FRAME:** The phone, the whole sample: four centuries in years After the Reckoning, the saga crowded at the right. A chip beside the ruler: "AR · your own calendar". A small note at the foot of the phone: "An invented world, made for this video".

**MOTION:** The phone rises in; the chip ticks in on "calendar".

**OST:** "Your world, your calendar"

**VO:**
"Put your whole world on one timeline, counted in your own calendar. Here, years after the Reckoning."

### Scene 3 · 0:12 – 0:20 · One thread

**FRAME:** The phone zooms into the saga; **Separate by tags** with `mirelle`: her blue circles lift into a track of their own (born at sea, the voyage, the map of the Drowned Coast, the escape).

**MOTION:** The tagged moments lift on "lifts"; each named step glows as it's said.

**OST:** "Pull out one thread"

**VO:**
"Separate by tags, choose Mirelle, and her whole story lifts into its own track: born at sea, the voyage, the map, the escape."

### Scene 4 · 0:20 – 0:27 · Move it

**FRAME:** Over the dimmed phone, the **Shift or scale times…** dialog, drawn as Video 6 draws it, applied to events tagged `mirelle`, shifting by one year. Her track slides a year to the right while the rest of the world stays put. An **Undo** keycap at the end.

**MOTION:** The dialog pops in; the track slides on "later"; the Undo keycap presses on "Undo".

**OST:** "Move it in one step"

**VO:**
"Need her a year later? Shift everything tagged Mirelle at once. One Undo puts it back."
