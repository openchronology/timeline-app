# OpenChronology — Short 1: 1776 to One Second on the Moon. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:27 · **Shape:** the zoom · **Look:** Archive · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** history and "did you know" feeds: curious newcomers and students. They don't know the app and won't wait for it; they stop for scale, a big span collapsing into one exact moment they've heard of.
**Purpose:** one continuous zoom on a phone, from the whole of the United States' history to the night of the Moon landing, to the second, so the app's one idea (any span, one exact line) is felt rather than explained.
**Points to:** Video 5, the United States tour.

**Production notes:**

- About 65 words, read briskly. The voice is delighted, not a lecturer's.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/usa.ochx`**, captured at phone size (480 × 640, scale 2.25) and shown in `phoneShot`.
- The zoom is one gesture across Scenes 1–3: the camera never cuts back out.

## ACT 1 · THE ZOOM (0:00 – 0:21)

### Scene 1 · 0:00 – 0:04 · All of it

**FRAME:** The phone, large in the frame, showing the whole sample after **Fit all**: 1775 at the left, 2008 at the right, summaries and bands along the line. Already whole at frame 0.

**MOTION:** None to start: the frame is the cover. The zoom begins as the sentence ends.

**OST:** "230 years → one second on the Moon"

**VO:**
"This is two hundred and thirty years of American history, on one line."

### Scene 2 · 0:04 – 0:11 · Keep going

**FRAME:** The same phone, the zoom running toward 1969: decades, then 1969, then July. A small span readout beside the phone counts down: "230 years", "10 years", "1 year", "1 week".

**MOTION:** A continuous zoom (log speed); the readout ticks as the view narrows.

**OST:** "230 years → one second on the Moon"

**VO:**
"Watch. Past the wars, past the decades, into one summer, one week, one night."

### Scene 3 · 0:11 – 0:21 · To the second

**FRAME:** The zoom settles on the night of 20–21 July 1969: "The Eagle has landed" and "First step on the Moon" apart on the line. Callouts beside the phone, in the look's serif: "20:17:40 UTC" for the landing, "02:56:15 UTC" for the first step.

**MOTION:** The zoom eases to a stop; each callout draws in on its time being said.

**OST:** "To the second"

**VO:**
"At 20:17:40 UTC, the Eagle lands. Six and a half hours later, the first step."
