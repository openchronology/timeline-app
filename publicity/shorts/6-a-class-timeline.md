# OpenChronology — Short 6: A Class Timeline Nobody Can Wreck. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:33 · **Shape:** the how-to · **Look:** Paper · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** teachers' feeds. A shared document a class can edit is a lesson in what goes wrong; the hook is the one thing they want and don't expect: everyone contributes, and nothing goes in without the teacher seeing it.
**Purpose:** name the fear (thirty students, one shared timeline), then three steps as interface cards: students propose, the teacher reviews and merges, the history keeps every save. End on sharing it with one link.
**Points to:** Video 9, working together.

**Production notes:**

- About 85 words. A teacher's colleague, not a salesperson: practical and quick.
- Drawn as interface cards, as Video 9 draws them (the role chips, the pull request with its additions and changes, **Merge** and **Reject**, the revision list); the timeline motif under them is the US sample's shape, not a capture.
- Be exact about accounts: contributors are added by their username (so they have an account on that server); **reading** a public timeline needs none.

## ACT 2 · THREE STEPS (0:05 – 0:28)

### Scene 2 · 0:05 – 0:12 · They propose

**FRAME:** The collaborators card: a student's username with the **Contributor** chip. Then the student's save button, which reads **Submit pull request**, and a pull request card: "Add: The Louisiana Purchase", "+1 moment", with a **Discussion** line under it.

**MOTION:** The chip ticks in on "contributors"; the button presses; the pull request card slides up on "proposal".

**OST:** "Students propose"

**VO:**
"Add your students as contributors. Their changes don't go straight in: each one becomes a proposal, showing exactly what it adds and changes."

### Scene 3 · 0:12 – 0:20 · You decide

**FRAME:** The pull request card, now with **Merge** and **Reject** buttons and a teacher's comment in the **Discussion**. The moment drops onto the timeline under it.

**MOTION:** The comment types in on "talk"; **Merge** presses on "merge"; the moment lands on the line.

**OST:** "You review"

**VO:**
"You talk it through, then merge it or reject it. Every save is kept in the history, so you can always go back."

### Scene 4 · 0:20 – 0:28 · One link

**FRAME:** The timeline, now public, and a link chip being copied; around it, a few phones and a laptop each showing the same timeline. A small chip: "no account needed to read".

**MOTION:** The link chip copies on "link"; the devices pop in around it.

**OST:** "Share one link"

**VO:**
"Make it public, and anyone can read it with one link, no account needed. Students can even fork their own copy."
