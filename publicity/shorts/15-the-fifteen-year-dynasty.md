# OpenChronology — Short 15: The Dynasty That Unified China Lasted 15 Years. Script and Storyboard

**Runtime:** ~0:42 · **Shape:** the zoom · **Look:** Archive · **Aspect:** 9:16
**For:** history and "did you know" feeds: curious newcomers and students. They know there was a First Emperor and a Great Wall; what stops them is a number that doesn't fit the legend.
**Purpose:** one surprising duration that the timeline makes visible at a glance: the Qin, which united China, is a sliver between the Warring States and the four centuries of the Han. Then the same shape again, eight hundred years later: the Sui and the Tang. It ends on the line that shows it.
**Points to:** Video 18, the China tour.

**Production notes:**

- About 115 words, read briskly. The voice is pleased with the pattern, not a lecturer's.
- **The timeline is the sample `publicity/video/samples/china.ochx`**, captured at phone size and shown in `phoneShot`. Capture narrow (360 × 480 CSS pixels at scale 3) and crop close to the bands, so the band names ("Warring States", "Qin", "Han", "Sui", "Tang") are readable on a phone; lower **Group within** to 12 px with an `eval` in each shot's `setup`.
- The callouts beside the phone carry the numbers; they point at bands and moments, never at the ruler's labels (BCE ruler marks read one year off, issue #340).
- **Accuracy:** Qin 221–206 BCE, Han 202 BCE–220 CE, Sui 581–618, Tang 618–907 (Appendix B). Say "about four hundred" for the Han (421 years, with Wang Mang's Xin between its halves) and "nearly three hundred" for the Tang (289).
