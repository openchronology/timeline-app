## What you can do

- **Teach from a public timeline.** Share its link, or put it on your course page with **Share → Embed on a website**. Students read it without signing in.
- **Build a class timeline together.** Add students under **Sharing & settings → Collaborators** as **Contributors**. Their edits arrive as pull requests, each showing what it adds, removes and changes, with a discussion. You **Merge** or **Reject** each one.
- **Show where each fact comes from.** Add the **Sources** and **Dating context** plugins, and a moment lists its sources and says how precise its date is: a day, or an estimate.
- **Make it look like a lesson.** The **History education** suite adds icons, background pictures for periods, rich text notes and headings in one step.
- **Work at any scale.** Show times as dates, years or millions of years, or in seconds down to fractions. Times are exact, so the formation of the Earth and a single second fit on the same line.
- **See what happened at the same time.** Compare timelines on one shared axis, or separate a timeline's moments by tags, such as `war` and `trade`, into tracks of their own.

## What you can count on

- **Free and open source.** OpenChronology is free software under the GNU GPL version 3, with no payments or paid features.
- **No account needed to read.** Anyone can read public timelines, and anyone can start a timeline in the browser and export it as a file.
- **Accounts are for people 13 and older.** Sign-up asks for a year of birth, and refuses anyone younger. Younger students can still read public timelines.
- **Private until you share.** A timeline saved to the server starts private: only you and the collaborators you add can open it.
- **Every save is kept.** Each save to the server is a revision, listed under **History**, that you can open and export.
- **Your school can run its own server.** Server packages for Windows, macOS, Debian, Ubuntu, Rocky Linux and RHEL, Fedora and FreeBSD, or Docker Compose on any system, keep your classes' timelines on your own machines. See [administration](/help/administration).
