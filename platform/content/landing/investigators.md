## What you can do

- **Chart events and causal factors.** The **Causal factor analysis** suite stacks contributing factors on a moment, tells kinds of factor apart by shape and color, draws conditions as ellipses and unconfirmed items dashed, and shows cause and effect with arrows.
- **Attach the evidence.** The **Sources** plugin keeps each moment's source links, and **Dating context** records how precise each time is.
- **Line up the clocks.** Compare timelines from different sources on one axis, with an offset for each, or use **Shift or scale times…** to correct a record made against the wrong clock.
- **Bring in the logs.** Import syslog, journald, Apache, nginx and JSON-lines logs at their exact times, next to the analysis.
- **Review each change.** Contributors propose changes as pull requests, and writers merge or reject them, each with its discussion.

## What you can count on

- **The history of every save.** Each save to the server is a revision, listed under **History**, that can be opened and exported.
- **Exact times.** Times are stored exactly, to any fraction of a second, and never rounded.
- **Private by default.** A timeline saved to the server is private until its owner shares it: only the collaborators they add can open it.
- **On your own machines.** Run your own server, or use the desktop app with files on your computer. Private timelines are hidden from other users, but not encrypted from the server's operator.
