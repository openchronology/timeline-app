## What you can do

- **Lay out the canon.** Moments for events, durations for reigns, journeys and ages, and links between the ones that belong together, with notes and pictures on each.
- **Follow one thread.** Tag events by character, book or storyline, then **Separate by tags** to lift them into a track of their own above the rest, and **Rejoin**.
- **Use your world's calendar.** A custom time display, a short script that prints and reads times, shows them in your world's own reckoning, such as years counted within each age.
- **Line up versions.** Compare a draft with the canon, or two books' timelines, on one axis.
- **Retime in one step.** **Shift or scale times…** moves or stretches everything, or only the events with certain tags.
- **Write with others.** Co-writers propose changes as pull requests, which you merge or reject, and every save is kept in **History**.

## What you can count on

- **Private until you publish.** Timelines saved to the server start private. Make one public when it's ready, and anyone can read it without an account.
- **Yours to keep.** Export any timeline as an `.ochx` file, or work offline in the desktop app or the single-file editor.
- **Free and open source,** under the GNU GPL version 3.
